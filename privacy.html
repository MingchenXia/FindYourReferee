Privacy policy

Effective August 23, 2026

What the service does

FindYourReferee is a remote MCP evidence-preparation service for authorship analysis in ChatGPT and Codex. The signed-in host model performs the reasoning. The service does not request or use an OpenAI API key.

Uploaded documents

Uploaded PDF, TXT, Markdown, and TeX files are downloaded from temporary host-provided URLs, processed in memory, and not intentionally stored. Extracted text, PDF metadata, and deterministic writing measurements are returned to the active conversation.

Public searches

Candidate names may be sent to public scholarly indexes such as arXiv to retrieve publication metadata. Private document text is not included in those search queries.

Logs and infrastructure

The hosting provider may retain ordinary security and operational logs, such as request time, error status, and network information. The application does not intentionally write uploaded document content or extracted text to logs.

Your choices

Do not upload material you are not authorized to process. You may omit private reference corpora, candidate background, PDF metadata, language habits, domain fit, or TeX evidence using the available controls.
