After setup

Three steps. That’s it.

  1. 1

    Upload the report

    Attach one or more PDF, TXT, Markdown, or TeX reports. You can also attach the manuscript being reviewed.

  2. 2

    Add possible authors

    Enter one candidate per line. Use a slash for different name forms, or leave the list empty to request candidate discovery.

  3. 3

    Start the analysis

    The model compares recurring errors, language, terminology, academic fit, metadata, and relevant public writing, then reports calibrated probabilities.

Example request

“Analyze this referee report. The possible authors are:
Name One
Name Two”

You may also provide private known-author samples and candidate background information. Results are evidence, not proof of identity.

What does the detailed report include?

Candidate probabilities in descending order, an outside-the-list probability, native-language and fluency signals, recurring spelling and grammar patterns, terminology and academic-background fit, PDF metadata, low-weight TeX habits, contrary evidence, and uncertainty.
